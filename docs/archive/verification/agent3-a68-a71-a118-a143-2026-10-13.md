## Decisions a reviewer should check

- **A-118 calls the engine, it does not re-implement it.** `threeWayReview()` calls the existing
  `performThreeWayMatch` exactly once and presents its results, so the report and the Purchase orders
  screen cannot disagree. The engine's own sentences are shown as its wording (they print a dollar
  sign); the columns beside them show the same figures in the order's own currency. The engine's
  fallback — matching a bill by vendor and amount within the tolerance when no order reference is
  recorded — is kept, and a test builds two orders of different amounts so the fallback is visible
  rather than assumed away.
- **A-68 never adds currencies.** MRR/ARR and every total are per currency. A plan is a recurring
  rule that carries an invoice template and a customer; ordinary recurring journals are left out.
  Weekly and daily plans are normalised to a month and marked approximate. A stopped plan's month is
  the month it last ran: the rule records a last occurrence, not a cancellation date, and the screen
  says so. A stopped plan with no occurrence is counted (`undatedStops`) instead of being dated.
- **A-143's projection is an assumption, visible on the screen.** Sales and cost reuse
  `computeSalesByItem` (issued invoices only, opening-balance documents out, posted cost preferred,
  the item-cost fallback marked estimated). Issued credit notes reduce revenue. The projected
  lifetime repeats the recorded average annual profit for a lifespan the reader types (1–20, default
  3); the screen says "an assumption … not a forecast" and shows the months of history behind it.
- **A-71 reuses `unitCostCents` from `vendorScorecard.ts`**, so the price history and the vendor
  scorecard cannot disagree about a unit cost. Draft, void, cancelled and rejected bills are not
  purchases. Prices are one series per vendor, item and currency; a single purchase shows no change
  percent rather than a made-up 0%.
