**Composition.** 9 English, 8 Hebrew, 3 mixed; 9 US and 11 Israeli receipts; USD and ILS.

**Hard cases the set holds (asserted by the test, so they cannot be dropped quietly):** discount before tax
(`r03`, `r15`); service charge before tax (`r11`); tip inside the total (`r02`); tax-included fuel (`r04`); explicit
zero tax (`r05`, `r06`); exempt dealer with no VAT (`r12`, `r17`); tax stated as a sum, not a rate (`r07`); cash
tendered and change outside the total (`r08`, `r17`); refundable deposit outside the total (`r09`); thousand
separators (`r13`, `r14`, `r16`); decimal quantities (weighed `r10`, `r16`; hourly `r13`); an ambiguous printed date
that only the country convention settles (`r18`, and `r14`'s `28/02`); two subtotal-looking labels on one paper
(`r18` and `r20`, Hebrew `סה"כ ביניים` with an English/last `TOTAL`); a zero-priced item line (`r09`).

**Answer key soundness.** The tests re-derive every fixture: each line amount equals quantity × unit price, the item
amounts equal the subtotal, the tax at the printed rate equals the printed tax, and the parts equal the total; the
transcription must show the merchant, the printed date, every item and the total; tendered minus total must equal
change; the ISO date must be one reading of the printed date and, when the reading is ambiguous, the one the receipt's
country means. Every image is checked to be a PNG whose IHDR size is the recorded size and whose SHA-256 is the
manifest's, so a swapped or truncated image fails the build rather than mislabelling an evaluation.

**Baseline measured (not a product claim).** `npm run eval:receipts`, feeding the app's own positional reader its
perfect transcription: merchant 12 correct / 8 wrong; the printed date 20/20; the calendar date not produced (20
missing); total 19/20 (the miss is `r17`, cash tendered larger than the amount paid with no English `total` label);
currency 20/20; **tax and items not read at all** (20 missing each). The eight merchant misses are one cause: a banner
line above the merchant name. This is what a fixture set is for — it names the failure modes instead of averaging them.
