## A-116 Inventory: kits assembled on demand (uses A-19 bundles)

A kit is an A-19 bundle (an item with parts) built out of its parts when it is needed — a bundle
never keeps stock of its own. New pure module `src/lib/kitAssembly.ts`:

- `kitBuildPlan(kit, items, N)`: each part's on-hand, per-kit quantity and how many kits it alone
  allows; `buildableKits` = the smallest; for N kits, the consumption (needed, would remain) and a
  plain-words problem when the parts fall short ("Only 2 kits can be built from what is on hand;
  Part A is short 1"). A part missing from the item list counts as none on hand and names itself —
  never invented stock.
- `kitBuildableSummary`: the "2 kits from stock; missing 2 Part A per kit" line for the list.
- `assembleOnDemand` mark on the item (next to `bundleParts`, only a bundle may carry it) —
  Settings-level note only; it decides nothing.

**Screens:** Reports → **Kit Assembly** (`KitAssemblyReportView.tsx`) — choose a kit, say how many,
see the consumption plan and CSV; the screen records nothing (no reservation, issue or posting).
Inventory list: a bundle's line shows how many kits its parts can build; the item dialog has the
"Assemble on demand" mark.

**Tests:** `kit-assembly.test.ts` (5), `kit-assembly-view.test.tsx` (2). Worked figures (hand):
Starter kit = 2 × Part A + 1 × Part B, on hand 5 and 3 → 2 kits; 3 kits take A 6/B 3 and fall one
Part A short.

## A-117 Inventory: stock valuation report by location and lot

New pure module `src/lib/stockValuation.ts` + Reports → **Stock Value by Location & Lot**
(`StockValueByLocationLotReportView.tsx`), an "As of" date, per-section CSV.

**By location** — the stock-moves ledger from the start of its history (the server's
`/reports/inventory-details`), valued exactly as Inventory Valuation values stock (`inventoryAsOf`,
ISS-131's verified method): an item its history does not explain is **unknown with the reason and
is never split across locations**; a known item's value is what its moves booked (moving weighted
average), summed per Location; moves without a location are "No location"; negative stock is valued
0 at item level (the existing rule) with a note when its location rows show what the moves booked.
Worked figures (hand, cents): bought 10 for 5,000 into L1, transferred 4 (2,000) L1→L2, sold 3
(1,500) from L1 → **L1 3 / 1,500; L2 4 / 2,000; item 7 / 3,500**.

**By lot** — goods receipts keep the lot, expiry and the price the goods arrived at, so a lot's
**received** quantity and value are known (worked: 10 @ 500 + 5 @ 600 = 15 received / 8,000,
average 533; 3 recorded at sale as a reference). The stock ledger does not track lots, so
**on-hand per lot is not known and is never computed** — the screen says so in its own line
(`LOT_ON_HAND_LIMIT`), the CSV's on-hand column says "not tracked", and the table is a
received-value table, not a stock-on-hand value. (A-13's limit, restated; closing it needs lots on
the stock moves — server work, flagged under Messages.)

**Tests:** `stock-valuation.test.ts` (5), `stock-value-by-location-lot-view.test.tsx` (2).
