### A-197 — Data Quality: Catalogue Items Without Cost or Selling Price
- **Library (`src/lib/dataQualityChecks.ts`):** `itemsMissingPricing` inspects catalogue items honestly by item kind:
  - **Stock items (`itemType !== 'service'` and not bundles):** flags missing/zero `unitCostCents` (causes $0 COGS on sale) or missing/zero `unitPriceCents` (leaves blank line price on quotes/invoices), noting on-hand quantity when stock is already held.

## 4. Bonus Launch Finding for AI #1 / AI #9 (`src/server/services/postingShadowCheck.ts` & `src/tests/l94-shadow-more.test.ts`)

Running the full PostgreSQL suite across the `00:00 UTC` midnight boundary (`20:00 America/New_York` on `2026-10-21` = `00:00 UTC` on `2026-10-22`) caught a two-part bug introduced in PR #296 (`L94-SHADOW-MORE`, untouched here per lane rules):
1. **Real evening mismatch bug (`postingShadowCheck.ts` vs client reversal date):** When reversing an entry in a locked period, the client builder stamps the reversal with local calendar date (`getLocalDateISO()` → `2026-10-21` in `America/New_York`), whereas the server shadow check stamps the expected reversal with UTC date (`new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)` → `2026-10-22`), reporting `matched: false` for US users every evening after 20:00 EDT / 19:00 EST.
2. **Hardcoded calendar date in test (`src/tests/l94-shadow-more.test.ts:145`):** Test 8 hardcodes `'2026-10-21'` as the expected reversal date for "reversed today", so on `2026-10-22` UTC every `UTC` CI run fails at line 145 (`expected ['2026-10-21|...', '2026-10-21|...']`, got `'2026-10-22|...'`).
