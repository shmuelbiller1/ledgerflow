# Receipt reading on Arena models (27 Nov 2026)

The 20 fixture receipts (`src/tests/fixtures/receipts/`, 9 English, 8 Hebrew, 3 mixed) were read through the
app's own reader (`readDocumentDraft`) on Arena, with the schema fix from #1057. Before that fix every request
failed with `400 invalid_json_schema`. Every answer is in `docs/eval/runs/ocr-2026-11-27.json`.

## What the misses really are

- **Dates:** almost all of these are dates like 06/03 or 08/05, which could be 6 March or 3 June. The reader
  is told to leave an ambiguous date empty, and the draft then asks the person to fill it in. That is the
  intended behaviour, not a wrong answer. No model wrote a wrong date.
- **Store name:** the misses are mixed Hebrew and English names written in the other word order, for
  example "עין בוקק Dead Sea Spa" for "Dead Sea Spa עין בוקק". The name is right, only the order differs.
- **Luna's only real miss:** the airline e-ticket (`r07`), where it returned no total, tax or currency.
  The draft marks those fields for review, so nothing wrong would be saved.

## Recommendation

Use **Luna** for receipts: `ASSISTANT_MODEL_OCR=gpt-6-luna`. It costs about 0.03¢ per receipt, 12 times
less than Gemini 3.8 Flash, and every one of its misses is left empty for the person to check rather than
filled in wrongly. Gemini 3.8 Flash read slightly more, but the app sends every `gemini-*` model straight to
Google, so it cannot be used through the Arena key today. Haiku 5.5 is not good enough for receipts.
