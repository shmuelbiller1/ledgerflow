- `src/lib/israeliBankCodes.ts` — the Bank of Israel identification codes a bookkeeper can meet
  today, read 2026-10-23 from BOI's supervised-banking-corporations list (reporting symbol =
  code + 001), BOI Appendix A ("List of identification codes", 2019, page updated 22 Sep 2023),
  and the later BOI allocation notices for nonbank payment service providers. 31 rows: 11 banks,
  4 foreign banks, 4 banking-group brands, Postal Bank (09), Masav (50) and Shva (59), Bank of
  Israel (99), 8 nonbank PSPs, and retired code 13 (Union Bank → Mizrahi Tefahot, 2022).
  Lookups accept the two-digit and the coming three-digit form (BOI, 13 Jan 2026: three digits
  from January 2027). An Israeli IBAN splits into bank (3) / branch (3) / account (13) through
  the shared mod-97 check (`ibanProblem`); bank/branch/account triples are checked in plain
  words. Unknown codes are "not in the table", never guessed; branch names/addresses are BOI's
  live branch list (linked), not a copied snapshot that would go stale.
- Reports → **Israeli Bank and Branch Codes** (`IsraeliBankCodesReportView.tsx`): search by code
  or English/Hebrew name, kind filter, IBAN checker, bank/branch/account checker, CSV with the
  source on every row. Tests: `israeli-bank-codes.test.ts` (7), `israeli-bank-codes-view.test.tsx` (1).
