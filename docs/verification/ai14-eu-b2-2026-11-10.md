# AI #14 — EU-B2 short document forms and PR #626 review follow-up

Date: 10 November 2026. Branch: `arena/57611a9e-ledgerflow`.
PR: https://github.com/shmuelbiller1/ledgerflow/pull/626 (EU-D1, EU-B1 and EU-B2).

## Implemented

- Invoice and bill forms start with party, date, due date, lines and tax. All other
  original controls remain mounted under a keyboard-accessible native **More options**
  disclosure. No field, validation, submit action or financial calculation was removed.
- Meaningful populated optional values open the section, including retainage,
  deposits, non-standard terms, currencies, recurring/progress settings and notes.
  Generated numbers and standard defaults do not expand a simple new form.
- The manual open/closed display preference is stored separately per person and
  form kind, not as document data. Storage failure does not prevent editing.
  Automatic opening does not overwrite a person's saved preference.
- Native required-field validation and custom document validation reveal the
  section before focusing the field. Collapsing never clears values.
- A prefilled bill's saved internal memo is restored from its existing metadata
  field so the note is both displayed and retained; no metadata schema changed.

  No screenshot baseline, tolerance or financial assertion was changed. Local screenshot

  Fonts); final visual parity must be established by CI, not claimed from that run.
