## A-72 — Statement of account by entity (World/Entity label)

**What a user gets.** Reports → Account Statement (also its standalone screen) gains an **Entity
label** select listing the labels this contact's own documents use, plus `(no entity label)` and
`All entities (whole account)`. Choosing one shows the opening balance, rows and running balance for
that label only; the printed statement names the entity in its header.

- `src/lib/statementByEntity.ts` (new): `entityLabelOf` reads the workspace's World/Entity dimension
  from a document's own `dimensions`, then from its items (invoices) or splits (bills);
  `entityOptions`, `matchesEntity`, `scopeStatementDocuments`, `entityStatementNote`.
- The scoped documents go into the **same** `computeStatement` the whole-account statement uses —
  one implementation, no second calculation of money.
- A posted payment is **copied, never changed**: its `amountCents` becomes the part allocated to this
  entity's documents. Money received but applied to no invoice of this entity has no entity yet, so
  it is left out and **counted on screen** ("1 posted payment ($300.00) allocated to no invoice of
  this entity is not in this statement").
- A credit note carries no labels of its own; it belongs to the entity of the invoice or bill it
  credits (`invoiceId`/`billId`).
- **Refused on purpose:** while an entity is chosen, *Preview PDF* and *Download PDF* are disabled
  with a stated reason. The server's `loadStatement` and the statement PDF take a date period only;
  showing a scoped screen beside an unscoped PDF is exactly what the 16 Oct warehouse note forbids.
  The whole-account PDF is unchanged when *All entities* is chosen.

**Worked figures** (`src/tests/statement-by-entity.test.ts`, 9 tests, every number by hand).
Customer Northwind: Entity A — invoice 1,000.00 on 10 Mar, paid 400.00 on 20 Apr, plus a 250.00
invoice on 1 Jan; Entity B — invoice 600.00 on 15 Mar; no label — invoice 90.00 on 5 Mar; a posted
1,000.00 payment allocated 400 to A and 600 to B; a posted 300.00 payment with no allocation.

| Scope | Opening | Billed | Paid | Closing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole account 1 Mar – 30 Apr | 250.00 | 1,690.00 | 1,300.00 | **640.00** |
| Entity A | 250.00 | 1,000.00 | 400.00 | **850.00** |
| Entity B | 0.00 | 600.00 | 600.00 | **0.00** |
| No entity label | 0.00 | 90.00 | 0.00 | **90.00** |

Also proven: the stored payment object is byte-identical after scoping, and a second scope still
reads its full 1,000.00.

**Open step for AI #1 (server lane):** an `entity`/label argument in `loadStatement`, the
`/reports/statement` route and the statement PDF route, so the PDF can print the same scope and the
two disabled buttons can be turned back on.

## A-218 — Print layout of P&L, balance sheet and trial balance (A4 / Letter, no cut columns)

- `src/lib/printLayout.ts` (new): A4 (210 × 297 mm) and US Letter (215.9 × 279.4 mm),
  `printPageRule()` → `@page { size: A4 portrait; margin: 12mm; }`, `PORTRAIT_COLUMN_LIMIT = 6`,
  `choosePrintOrientation()`, `printableWidthMm()` (A4 186 mm, A4 landscape 273 mm, Letter 191.9 mm,
  Letter landscape 255.4 mm), `countPrintedColumns()`, and the choice remembered per browser
  (`lf_print_paper`, default A4).
- `src/components/reporting/PrintLayoutControl.tsx` (new): **Print on** select beside *Print / PDF*
  on the Reports page (where P&L, balance sheet and trial balance live). It injects the `@page` rule
  while mounted, states the sheet and printed width ("A4 portrait · 186 mm wide"), and turns the
  sheet when the table is wider: the Reports page measures the widest header row **when Print is
  pressed** and says "wide table turned so no column is cut".
- `src/index.css` print block: navigation, header and buttons still hidden (A-17/Y-19 behaviour kept);
  every scroll box prints in full (`overflow: visible`), tables print at 100 % width with
  `table-layout: auto`, a long account name wraps inside its cell (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), amounts
  keep one line (`white-space: nowrap`), and rows/headings do not split across pages.
- `src/tests/print-layout.test.ts` (9 tests): measurements, the exact `@page` text per sheet and turn,
  landscape above 6 columns, printed widths worked by hand, persistence, the injected rule appearing
  and disappearing with the control, column measurement, and the print stylesheet rules above.
